About

Engineering trust into every layer of the network.

From the cable in the rack to the firewall policy and the boardroom report — I make infrastructure reliable, secure and understood.

My work sits at the intersection of network engineering, cybersecurity and customer trust. I design, deploy and integrate enterprise systems — networks, firewalls, servers, storage, virtualization and cloud — then harden them against real-world threats and keep them running through monitoring, backup and disaster recovery.
